# 💖 5 Years of Us • Kunjutan & Kunjuti (Basil & Nandini) ❤️
### Celebrating 5 Magical Years of Relationship & Endless Love

An emotional, interactive, mobile-first 5-year relationship anniversary web experience celebrating half a decade of love, laughter, and growing together.

---

## 🔒 Private Passcode Protection
- **Secret Passcode**: `511303`
- The private lock screen requests the 6-digit passcode.
- Entering `511303` unlocks the 5-year journey with golden starlight confetti and romantic music!

---

## 🌟 Interactive Chapters & Tasks to Unlock
1. **Prologue**: "5 Years of Us • Kunjutan & Kunjuti" with romantic love quotes and sound toggle.
2. **Task 1 (Starlight Destiny)**: Connect the cosmic stars in sequence to bind Basil & Nandini's 5-year link.
3. **Task 2 (Cupid's Resonance)**: Pull back the golden bow and shoot straight into Kunjutan's beating heart.
4. **Task 3 (Relationship Q&A Quiz)**: Answer 4 sweet couple trivia questions revealing secret notes and quotes.
5. **Task 4 (5-Year Memory Vault)**: 25 photos organized across Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, Year 4, and Year 5 with tabs and swipe gestures.
6. **Task 5 (5 Lanterns of 5 Years)**: Release 5 glowing sky lanterns into the night sky, each holding a deep reflection for that year.
7. **Task 6 (5 Candles for 5 Years)**: 5 glowing candles on the cake. Make an anniversary wish and blow them out!
8. **Task 7 (Wax-Sealed Love Letter)**: 3D envelope with custom wax seal `B❤️N` opening into Kunjutan's 5-year love letter.
9. **Task 8 (Grand Finale & Forever Promise)**: Interactive heart fireworks on tap + "Will you walk the next 50 years with me, Kunjuti? 💍".

## 📸 How to Add Your 25 Photos (5 Per Year)
1. Add your photos to the `assets/photos/` folder:
   - **Year 1**: `y1_1.jpg`, `y1_2.jpg`, `y1_3.jpg`, `y1_4.jpg`, `y1_5.jpg`
   - **Year 2**: `y2_1.jpg`, `y2_2.jpg`, `y2_3.jpg`, `y2_4.jpg`, `y2_5.jpg`
   - **Year 3**: `y3_1.jpg`, `y3_2.jpg`, `y3_3.jpg`, `y3_4.jpg`, `y3_5.jpg`
   - **Year 4**: `y4_1.jpg`, `y4_2.jpg`, `y4_3.jpg`, `y4_4.jpg`, `y4_5.jpg`
   - **Year 5**: `y5_1.jpg`, `y5_2.jpg`, `y5_3.jpg`, `y5_4.jpg`, `y5_5.jpg`
2. In `config.js`, change the file extensions from `.svg` to `.jpg` under `yearsData` (or simply tell me here and I will update it for you!).
